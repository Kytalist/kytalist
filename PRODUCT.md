# Product

## Register

brand

## Users

Kytalist serves students exploring extracurricular programs, competitions, internships, research opportunities, camps, conferences, exchange programs, and other high-signal experiences. They arrive with limited time, many open tabs, and a need to compare eligibility, deadlines, cost, region, and category without getting lost.

## Product Purpose

Kytalist turns scattered student-opportunity data into one calm, curated catalog. Success means visitors can quickly understand the breadth of opportunities, trust the curation, and move from the home page into an all-events or filtered listing path with confidence.

## Brand Personality

Calm, exacting, and encouraging. The brand should feel like a well-organized advisor: warm enough for students, structured enough for serious planning, and distinctive enough to feel curated rather than scraped.

## Anti-references

Avoid generic SaaS hero templates, noisy dashboard chrome, exaggerated AI glassmorphism, purple-gradient startup aesthetics, and dense admin-style data tables on the public site. Do not make the experience feel like homework, a job board clone, or a generic education marketplace.

## Design Principles

1. Lead with clarity: every major surface should make the next useful action obvious.
2. Make breadth feel manageable: show many paths without overwhelming students.
3. Preserve calm momentum: use spacious hierarchy, confident contrast, and minimal friction.
4. Show the product, not decoration: visual flourishes should communicate discovery, filtering, or deadlines.
5. Stay curated: copy and layout should feel selected and intentional, never scraped or template-like.

## Accessibility & Inclusion

Target WCAG AA contrast and keyboard-accessible interactions. Motion should respect reduced-motion preferences and never hide essential content. Public copy should be direct, student-friendly, and understandable without insider admissions or career jargon.
